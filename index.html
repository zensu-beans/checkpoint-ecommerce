Good to know

Help & FAQs

Quick answers about at-home fittings, pricing and extra services.

How much does a fitting cost?

Delivery and the at-home fitting are ₱150 per appointment.

Can I try more than one size or style?

Yes. Try multiple sizes or styles during your appointment and keep the pair that fits best.

When will your team contact me?

Our team will contact you 2 hours before your scheduled time.

Which cities can I book in?

The booking form currently lists Quezon City, Makati, Pasig and Taguig.

What extra services are available?

Home shoe cleaning (₱350 per pair, free for the first 20 bookings), shoe polishing (₱150 per pair), repair pickup (₱150 per transaction, repair fees are separate) and other services at ₱100–₱300 depending on service, materials and labor.
